# Privacy Policy — Frescovo

_Last updated: 2026-08-19_

Frescovo ("the app", "we", "us") helps you track expiry dates of items in your home, get
AI-assisted recipe suggestions, and recognize items and receipts from photos. This page explains
what data we collect, how we use it, where it is stored, and what your choices are.

By using the app, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the
app.

## 1. Data we collect

| Data type | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Item information | Item name, expiry date, quantity, unit, category, storage location (fridge/freezer/pantry), price (if entered manually or scanned from a receipt) |
| Usage history | Items marked as consumed or wasted, used to build waste-reduction statistics |
| Photos | Photos of items or receipts you choose to take for automatic recognition |
| Account information | When signing in with Google: your email, display name, and Google account identifier |
| Shared fridge | The 6-character invite code when you create or join a shared-fridge group |
| Purchases | Purchase records for AI credit packs bought through Google Play (product, transaction token, credits granted) |
| App activity | Which app features you use and when, recorded as a request log (your account identifier, the feature called, a status code, and a timestamp) |

We collect only what the features above require. We do **not** collect your contacts, location,
call logs, SMS, or the contents of other apps.

The app is **usable immediately without signing in** — only AI features (photo recognition,
recipe suggestions) and shared-fridge features require Google sign-in.

## 2. Why we collect it

- To provide the features you ask for (expiry tracking, sync, recognition, suggestions).
- To keep the service running reliably and to detect abuse or technical faults.
- To honour purchases you make and to prevent duplicate or fraudulent transactions.

We do **not** use your data for advertising, and we do **not** build advertising profiles. The
app contains no third-party advertising SDKs.

## 3. Where data is sent

- **Photos** you submit are sent to the **Google Gemini API** for recognition and suggestion.
  We **do not store photos** in our database or file storage — they are forwarded for
  processing and discarded once the result is returned. Google processes this data under
  [Google's own terms and privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), which we do not
  control.
- **Sign-in information** (email, name, account identifier) is provided by **Google Sign-In**
  when you choose to sign in.
- **Purchases** are processed by **Google Play Billing**. We never see, receive, or store your
  card number, bank details, or any payment credentials — we receive only a confirmation that a
  purchase is valid.

## 4. Where data is stored, and for how long

- **On your device:** a local database, so the app works offline.
- **On our server:** a hosted PostgreSQL database, used to sync your data across your own
  devices and between members of a shared fridge. Our infrastructure is provided by third-party
  cloud providers (Amazon Web Services and Neon).

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, the
associated records are deleted as described in section 7. Aggregate, non-identifying statistics
(for example, total number of requests per day) may be retained.

## 5. Who data is shared with

- **Members of the same "shared fridge"** you have joined can see the shared item list. This is
  an intentional feature you opt into by creating or entering an invite code — please share
  invite codes only with people you trust.
- Beyond that, we **do not sell** your data and **do not share** it with third parties, other
  than the service providers named in this policy (Google, and our cloud hosting providers)
  strictly for the purposes described here.
- We may disclose data if required to do so by law, by a valid legal request, or where necessary
  to protect our rights, safety, or property.

## 6. Security

We use industry-standard measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS), token-based
authentication, and access controls. However, **no method of transmission or storage is
completely secure**, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping
your device and your Google account credentials secure.

## 7. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all associated data (items, usage history, purchased AI credits,
sign-in information, activity records) directly in the app: **Profile → Delete account**.

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot sign in, see the instructions at
[chechle39.github.io/fridge-guardian-legal/delete-account/](https://chechle39.github.io/fridge-guardian-legal/delete-account/).

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Purchased AI credits are forfeited on deletion and
are not refundable.

## 8. Children

The app is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13 (or the
minimum age of digital consent in your country, whichever is higher). We do not knowingly
collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we
will delete it.

## 9. International users

The app is operated from Vietnam and your data may be processed and stored on servers located
outside your country of residence. By using the app, you consent to this transfer and
processing.
