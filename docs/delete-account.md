# Delete Account & Data — Frescovo

_Last updated: 2026-08-17_

This page explains how to permanently delete your Frescovo account and all associated data.
Deletion **cannot be undone**.

## Data that will be deleted

- All fridge items (name, expiry date, quantity, price, recognized photos)
- Usage history (consumed/wasted, waste-reduction statistics)
- Purchased AI analysis credits (unused credits are lost, no refund)
- Sign-in information (email, display name, Google account identifier)
- Purchase records for AI credit packs
- App activity records (which features you used and when)
- Shared-fridge membership (if you're part of a group)

## Option 1 — Delete directly in the app (fastest)

1. Open the Frescovo app and sign in with Google if you haven't already.
2. Go to the **Profile** tab.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm in the dialog — your data is deleted immediately.
